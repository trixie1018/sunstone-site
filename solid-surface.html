Solid Surface

Sunstone's acrylic solid surfaces are created from resins infused with Alumina-Trihydrate and other specialized components, resulting in a durable, non-porous material. Available in a variety of patterns such as solid, sanded, marble, travertine, and others. Sunstone's solid surface countertops feature basic modern designs, easy maintenance, and durability - ideal for residential and commercial uses.

Seamless Joints

invisible joint lines create a unified, cohesive surface

Heat Resistant

withstands high temperatures from kitchen cookware

Stain Resistant

resistant to absorption of sauces

Easy Maintenance

only requires simple wiping or cleaning with soap and water

Durable

engineered for long-lasting performance

Repairable Surface

scratches can be erased through buffing and polishing
