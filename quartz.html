Quartz

Sunstone's engineered quartz is another popular alternative to solid surface countertops due to its similar properties. Engineered quartz are composite material made of crushed natural quartz stone, bound together with an adhesive. Like solid surfaces, quartz is also non-porous and durable, making it highly resistant to heat and everyday stains. It combines the design variety of natural stone with the resilience and durability of engineered materials.
